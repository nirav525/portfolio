## The problem

Forecast error was being measured as a single number, which says how wrong the plan was and nothing about why. Meanwhile inventory that would not sell through before expiry was discovered when it expired.

## Why it mattered

Excess and obsolete stock is capital and shelf space already spent. Once a lot is past its recovery window it is a pure loss; a few weeks earlier the same lot could be renegotiated with a vendor, redirected, or moved at a discount.

## What I built

Automated planning analytics and variance reporting that decomposed forecast error by SKU and cause, so the fix could target the actual driver. On top of it, a redesigned shelf-life process with an early trigger for offloading inventory that current velocity would not sell before expiry, paired with vendor contract negotiation. Excess and obsolete inventory fell 29%, and $300K of at-risk stock was recovered.
