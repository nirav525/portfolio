## The problem

A fast-moving startup with no operating rhythm. Priorities were set verbally, blockers surfaced late, and nothing converted strategic intent into assigned, dated work.

## Why it mattered

Below a certain size informal coordination works fine. Past it, the absence of a cadence means the same blockers get rediscovered every week and priorities drift without anyone deciding they should.

## What I built

The weekly operating cadence — surfacing blockers, assigning owners and milestones, tracking commitments — and the process that translates company priorities into quarterly execution plans. I also produce the operations content for board and investor materials.

This is the hardest project here to demonstrate, and I have not tried to invent an interactive version of it. It is on the site because it is the most senior-scope work I do, and leaving it off would misrepresent the role.
